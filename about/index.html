What Openshelf is, and what it is not

Openshelf collects research that people publish on their own websites, such as personal pages, lab pages and company research blogs, and checks that each item is a research report or a careful explainer.

Inclusion is not peer review and not an endorsement. Responses are linked so that readers can follow the discussion, not to deliver a verdict.

Points and dates are snapshots taken during the crawl. Titles and texts belong to their authors; Openshelf keeps only metadata and a one-line summary.

To suggest a post, correct a label or ask for an item to be removed: https://github.com/chenxiachan/openshelf/issues.

Openshelf is maintained by Xia Chen (GitHub).
